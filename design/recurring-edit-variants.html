Editing a recurring charge, three treatments
Working previews on the app's own juniper.css. Your two real streams, with two changes already applied in all three: the Chase institution mark on the annual fee, and the shouting description title-cased. Click Edit, a row, or Confirm to try each one.
One constraint shapes all three. recurring_overrides.state is NOT NULL and the endpoint only accepts confirm or dismiss, so an amount cannot be saved without also confirming or dismissing the stream. A and B therefore offer Edit on confirmed rows only. C folds the correction into the Confirm itself, which is the one treatment that needs no migration to cover a pending row.
Also visible here: the head reads $15.00 while the rows are $7.92 and $6.98, which sum to $14.90. sumMonthly rounds the total to whole dollars and then renders it with cents, so it claims a precision it does not have.
AInline on the row

Why: The control sits on the number it changes, the same principle as the category picker in #201. Two fields is few enough to fit the row, and nothing moves on the page.
Cost: The row is crowded at this width and wraps on mobile, where .sub-row already sends buttons to their own line. Rename and the revert affordance have nowhere to go.
