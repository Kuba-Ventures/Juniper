The bug: a member with both an onboarding profile and linked Plaid sees the profile-derived score (53) on first paint, then the live one (97). Two different bands. The question these three answer: what the score area shows in the window before the live fetch resolves. Each is drawn twice, at first paint and settled, because the whole point is what happens between them. The manual-only member is unaffected in all three: their profile score IS the answer, and it still paints immediately.
C was chosen and is what shipped. Two things the build found that this mock did not predict: the card height was driven by the trend head's delta chip, not by the paragraph, so the chip keeps its place while pending; and the trend spark and its start value were still drawn from the manual layer, so a dashed "now" sat beside a manual "39". Both are withheld now.
