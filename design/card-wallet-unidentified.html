The wallet draws the cards still to be identified
Reverses a deliberate decision in rewards-guide.tsx, whose docblock said an outline in the pocket would read as a rendering fault. The evidence came back against it: the header says "2 of 3 cards identified, 1 still to go" and a real member read that against a stack of two as their Chase card having gone missing. The outline is labelled and tappable, which is what makes it a prompt rather than a fault. It is drawn last, which in this layout means at the FRONT and fully visible rather than tucked away: the outline is the only slot in the pocket with something to do, so it earns that place, and putting it earlier would push the member's real cards down behind an unanswered one.
