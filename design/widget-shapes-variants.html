Widget shapes — how a member picks a size, in arrange mode
Issue #259: a widget can declare more than one size it honestly draws at (the Score is a ring at half width, the same factor rails from /app/score at full width), and the arrange mode already open on this page is where a member chooses. These three differ only in the CONTROL, not in what either size looks like — click "Full" in any column to see the Score widget actually switch, the same swap the real page will do.
ACorner toggle

A third round button beside the grip and the remove badge, matching their exact size and shape. Click cycles to the next declared size. Cheapest to add, reads as "one more control in the set already there," and needs no label since an icon-only affordance is the pattern the grip and remove already use.

Current: half
BLabeled segmented pill

A pill docked at the top edge of the card, reusing the exact .pills control the net-worth range already uses. Names the sizes in the member's words ("Ring" / "Full breakdown") rather than an icon, so nobody has to guess what cycling does, at the cost of being the widest control of the three and only fitting a card wide enough to hold it.

Current: half
CDropdown menu

A chevron button beside the grip opens a small menu listing every declared size with a check on the active one. The only one of the three that scales past two sizes without getting wider or needing a second cycle-click, which matters once a widget other than the Score declares a third shape.

Current: half
