Card rewards on the Credit page, three treatments (issue #168)
Same data behind all three: four linked credit accounts, the member has confirmed which product each one is, and ninety days of their own categorized spending. What differs is what the page leads with and how it is organized. Pick one and it gets built.
Two things are deliberately different from the Credit Karma screenshots on the issue, and they are the same in all three treatments.
1. No issuer card art. Those images are trademarked and Credit Karma licenses them. Every card face below is synthesized from the product's stored brand colour plus the issuer's own logo as served by Plaid, which Juniper already renders on Connections and on Credit today. 2. Comparing a points card against a cash-back card is an assumption, and it is labelled as one. "3x points" is only worth more than "2% cash back" if a point is worth something, so the house valuation travels with every figure that used it and the surface says so. Credit Karma never cross-compares currencies, which is how it avoids owing anybody that disclosure.
A Stacked sections, closest to the screenshots
The shape Credit Karma uses, in Juniper's own design system: a card-art hero with the totals, then the earning guide as a category list, then the benefits tracker grouped by perk family, then the upgrade ideas. One scroll on /app/credit, under the existing utilization card. Organized by category, which answers "what do I reach for at the pump" and is the question people actually have at the till. Everything above depends on knowing which product each linked account is, and Plaid does not say. It returns an institution and an account name, and that name is routinely "CREDIT CARD" or "Card ending 4021". So the member confirms, once per card, and nothing on this surface appears until they have. There is no auto-confirm path, deliberately: a wrong guess attaches a stranger's reward rates to somebody's real spending and then quotes them a confident dollar figure off it. Juniper ranks the guesses and the member taps.
