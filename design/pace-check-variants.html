The bug: "On track" was never a computed verdict — it is the label viewOf() prints whenever a plan has a target and a monthly amount set, with no comparison to the target date at all (pages/app/plans.tsx:299-322). The fix being wired: when a plan carries an explicit target date, project a finish date from the stated monthly amount (monthsToClose, already in lib/plans.ts) and compare it to that date. No target date means nothing to be behind on, so those plans keep reading "On track" exactly as before — this never invents a deadline the member did not set, matching every other pace calculation in this app. These three answer only the presentation question: how a plan that IS behind should look. Two real plans below, "Individual" ($29,777 of $10,000,000 at $600/mo, target Dec 2033 — projected 2827) and "Travel" ($8,873 of $20,000 at $600/mo, target Dec 2029 — on pace), so each column shows both the changed state and the unchanged one side by side.
