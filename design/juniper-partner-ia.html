Recommendation

Option 1 — the "Shared" tab — with a light touch of Option 2

For an individual-first product it's the cleanest fit: your private app never changes, and Shared appears as a single, clearly-scoped destination only once a partner connects. It's the most discoverable and avoids "which mode am I in?" confusion. Inside the Shared tab we use the B2 design with its own sub-tabs (Overview · Bills · Activity · Sharing) — that borrows Option 2's scalability within the shared surface without splitting the whole app into two navigations. Invite stays reachable from the account menu + Plans. Option 3's toggle is a nice future accent (a "＋ Devin" peek on Home) but shouldn't be the primary access.
