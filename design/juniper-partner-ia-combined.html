✓ Why this combination works

The two controls never compete: the switcher answers “whose money am I looking at?” and the avatar menu answers “my account & actions.” You keep Option 2's clean "our money" mode with its own tabs (Overview · Accounts · Goals · Bills · Activity), and Option 1's familiar profile dropdown where Invite partner naturally lives. Solo users only ever see "My finances" in the switcher — the shared workspace appears the moment a partner connects, and disconnecting removes it. Invite is reachable from both the avatar menu and the Plans panel.
