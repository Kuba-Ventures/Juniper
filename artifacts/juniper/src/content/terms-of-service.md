## 1. What Juniper is

Juniper ("Juniper," "we," "us") is a financial planning application that connects to a member's
own bank, credit card, investment, and loan accounts (via Plaid) to show net worth, spending,
budgets, a proprietary 0–100 Juniper Score, and financial plans, and that offers an AI assistant
("Ask Juniper") for financial guidance grounded in the member's own data.

**Juniper is not a bank, a broker-dealer, an investment adviser, a credit repair organization, or a
licensed financial, tax, or legal advisor.** Nothing in the app, including anything "Ask Juniper"
says, is individualized financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. See Section 8.

## 4. Linked accounts and third-party data

Juniper uses **Plaid** to connect to your financial institutions. When you link an account, Plaid
shares your account and transaction data with Juniper under Plaid's own end-user terms and privacy
policy, which you accept separately when you link an account through Plaid's interface. Juniper
never sees or stores your bank login credentials; only Plaid does.

Juniper may also connect to **Spinwheel** to pull a credit-bureau score and factors, gated on your
explicit, separate consent (a checkbox plus a real SMS one-time-passcode verification of your phone
number and date of birth). See the Privacy Policy, Section 4, for what is and is not retained from
that flow.

You are responsible for the accuracy of any account you connect and for any data you enter
manually (a hand-typed balance, credit limit, or account).

## 5. The Juniper Score is not a credit score

The Juniper Score (0-100) is Juniper's own proprietary measure, built from your savings rate,
emergency fund, debt load, investing pace, and, where you've connected it, your real bureau
credit score as one factor among several. **It is not a FICO® Score, a VantageScore®, or any
score used by a lender to make a credit decision, and lenders do not see or use the Juniper
Score.** Where Juniper displays a real bureau score (currently sandbox/test data only, see the
"Sandbox test data" disclosure on the Credit page), that score is provided by our credit-data
provider and may differ from the score any given lender uses.

## 6. Ask Juniper: AI-generated content

"Ask Juniper" uses a third-party large language model (Anthropic's Claude) to answer questions
grounded in your own linked financial data. Responses are generated by an AI system and:

- are **not** individualized financial, investment, tax, or legal advice, and Juniper is not a
  licensed financial advisor, insurance agent, mortgage broker, CPA, or attorney;
- should be confirmed with a licensed professional before you act on anything involving a signed
  policy, a locked rate, taxes, or legal exposure;
- may be inaccurate. You are responsible for verifying anything before relying on it.

If a conversation touches signs of crisis (for example, mentions of self-harm), Ask Juniper is
designed to set the financial question aside and point you to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
and to people in your life. **Ask Juniper is not a crisis service, a therapist, or a substitute
for professional mental-health care.**

By using Ask Juniper, you acknowledge your messages and the account data used to ground them are
sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response, subject to Anthropic's own commercial API terms.
Anthropic does not use API inputs or outputs to train its models, and retains them only as needed
for abuse detection and legal compliance, for a limited period, under those terms.
